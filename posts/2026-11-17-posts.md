## Post 2 — 17:00 — image: post2.png
Setting up my AI poster taught me one rule:

Before you add a setting, search for it.

Twice, the file already had the line. I added a second one. The program then ignored the whole file.

The fix took one minute. Finding the cause took an hour.

Search first. Edit the line that exists.

What is the small mistake that cost you the most time?
