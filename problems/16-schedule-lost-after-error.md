# A post failed and vanished from the calendar

**Why:** When a publish fails, Postiz shows an error notification (bell) and the post may not stay on the calendar as you expect.

**Fix:** Check the bell and the **Published** tab in list view. Ask Hermes to re-create the post for a time a few minutes ahead (same text and picture), and to report the exact error if it fails again.

**Watch out:** Fix the cause first (#13, #14). Re-posting without fixing it just fails again.
